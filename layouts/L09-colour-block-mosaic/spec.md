# Colour Block Mosaic

**Visual test:** The format is divided into three to five large rectangles of flat saturated colour, with photos and type placed in or against the blocks.

## Layout
- **Structure:** Unequal flat-colour panels tiling the format (Mondrian-like); one panel holds a photo, others hold headline, links or data.
- **Hierarchy:** Colour weight drives hierarchy; the largest or brightest block leads.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: each section is a new arrangement of colour panels. Across a site: a fixed palette of panels recombines per page.
- **Web first screen:** Viewport tiled into 3–5 flat colour panels of unequal size, one with a photo, others with headline and links.

## Tone
Playful, modernist, confident. Saturated primaries or a tuned 3–4 colour set, grotesk type. Pairs with: Systems Modernism, Pop Conversation Tabloid, Bento Container.

## Not
Not Split Field (only two). Not Exposed Ruled Grid (lines, neutral). Not bento cards (rounded, gapped, product UI).
