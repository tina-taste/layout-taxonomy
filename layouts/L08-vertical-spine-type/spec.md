# Vertical Spine Type

**Visual test:** A line of type is rotated 90° and runs the full height of the format as a structural column or edge, with other content hung against it.

## Layout
- **Structure:** One vertical type column (left, right or centre) the full height of the format; remaining content arranged against it in the other columns.
- **Hierarchy:** The spine is the anchor; it organizes rather than decorates.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: the spine stays fixed at the viewport edge while content scrolls beside it. Across a site: the spine carries the section or brand name on every page.
- **Web first screen:** A rotated wordmark or section title fixed down the left or right edge, content scrolling beside it.

## Tone
Bold, editorial, sporty or cultural-event. Condensed grotesk, strong contrast, flat colour. Pairs with: Late Swiss New Wave, Expressive Typographic, Pop Conversation Tabloid.

## Not
Not Type on a Path (diagonal/curved, expressive). Not a sidebar nav (the spine is display type, not links).
