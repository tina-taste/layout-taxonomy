# Ledger Composition

**Visual test:** The whole composition is a dense list, table or index set in columns and rows; the rhythm of the text replaces an image.

## Layout
- **Structure:** Rows of entries (title / year / client / category) in aligned columns, separated by rules or spacing. Little or no imagery; images appear only on hover or as tiny inline thumbs.
- **Hierarchy:** Uniform row weight; hierarchy by column position and a single larger title or masthead.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: the list continues; sections are grouped headers. Across a site: index pages are lists, detail pages keep the tabular metadata.
- **Web first screen:** A full-screen index: rows of project/year/client in mono or grotesk, rules between, no hero image.

## Tone
Cool, archival, insider. Monochrome, monospace or grotesk at one or two sizes, generous line rhythm. Pairs with: Information Design, Austere Academic, Neo-Swiss Archive.

## Not
Not Text Wall (running prose). Not Exposed Ruled Grid (mixed content in boxes).
