# Text Wall

**Visual test:** A block of running prose is set large enough to fill most of the format like a texture; any image is small or secondary.

## Layout
- **Structure:** One large text block (justified or ragged) occupying most of the frame, cropping at an edge; small image or caption tucked in.
- **Hierarchy:** The paragraph is the hero; no separate headline.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: the manifesto continues at display size; sections are new paragraphs. Across a site: about/statement pages lead with the wall.
- **Web first screen:** A manifesto paragraph at ~4–6vw filling the viewport and cropping off the bottom edge, no image.

## Tone
Declarative, intellectual, voice-led. Neutral ground, one grotesk or serif at large text size. Pairs with: Literary Quarterly, Critical Contemporary, Academic Deconstruction.

## Not
Not Ledger Composition (tabular). Not Monumental Type Fill (a few words, not prose).
