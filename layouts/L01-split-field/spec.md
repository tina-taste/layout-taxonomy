# Split Field

**Visual test:** The page is cut into two contrasting fields along one straight line (photo vs flat colour, image vs text, black vs white); neither half is subordinate.

## Layout
- **Structure:** Two full-height (or full-width) regions, usually 50/50 or 60/40. One region carries image, the other carries type/navigation. The cut line is the strongest element on the page.
- **Hierarchy:** Equal weight across the halves; headline sits in the non-image half, aligned to the cut.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: alternating split sections (image left/text right, then swap), or one half stays fixed while the other scrolls. Across a site: every page keeps the two-field division.
- **Web first screen:** 50/50 viewport: full-height photo one side, flat colour field with headline on the other.

## Tone
Confident, graphic, editorial-commercial. Saturated flat colour against photography, or strict black/white. Grotesk display type. Pairs with: Cinematic Spread, Strict Modular Swiss, Glossy Conceptual.

## Not
Not Colour Block Mosaic (3+ panels). Not a sidebar layout (the sidebar is subordinate; here both halves are equal).
