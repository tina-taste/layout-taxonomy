# Specimen Matrix

**Visual test:** Ten or more equal-sized images in a regular grid, like a contact sheet or specimen tray; the matrix itself is the composition.

## Layout
- **Structure:** Uniform grid of same-size cells, edge to edge or with even gutters. Minimal labels under or inside cells. No hero.
- **Hierarchy:** Deliberately flat; the eye reads the set, not the item.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: the grid continues (infinite catalogue). Across a site: collection/archive pages are matrices; detail pages enlarge one specimen.
- **Web first screen:** Edge-to-edge grid of uniform thumbnails (products, faces, objects) with tiny labels and nothing else above the fold.

## Tone
Typological, collector, deadpan. White or neutral ground, consistent photography (same light, same crop), small grotesk captions. Pairs with: Neo-Swiss Archive, Drifting Photography Grid, Photo-Essay.

## Not
Not Motif Field (one repeated graphic). Not Exposed Ruled Grid (visible rules, mixed content). Not masonry (unequal sizes).
