# Whisper Margins

**Visual test:** The centre is empty or nearly empty and all information is tiny type pushed into the corners and edges; the frame is the composition.

## Layout
- **Structure:** Four-corner or edge-anchored text blocks (name, nav, date, contact) around a large empty field; optionally one small dot or image.
- **Hierarchy:** Inverted: the biggest element is empty space; type is all one small size.
- **How it extends (page + site):** Down a page: each section is mostly empty with edge-anchored captions; content appears small and isolated. Across a site: the corner furniture stays fixed on every page.
- **Web first screen:** An almost blank viewport with four tiny text fragments at the corners (name, nav, year, contact).

## Tone
Hushed, gallery, conceptual, expensive. White or off-white ground, small grotesk or mono, no colour. Pairs with: Dutch Reductive, Critical Contemporary, Austere Academic.

## Not
Not Lone Object in Void (that has a centred focal object). Not generic minimalism (here the edges are actively used).
