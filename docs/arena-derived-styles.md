# Arena-derived compositional styles

Source: Are.na board "GD – Layout, Format & Composition". 694 pages (p006–p699), read across all 29 contact sheets. Each image was tagged by its main **compositional move**: how space, type, image and grid are arranged. Subject, colour, medium and era were ignored. Counts are approximate (±15%) because they come from thumbnails. An image can count toward a secondary style, so the counts add up to more than 694.

## What the board is like overall

- **Mostly print, documented.** About 35% are posters (flat or photographed in situ), about 25% are editorial spreads and book covers, about 10% are identity pieces (cards, stationery, sticker sheets, packaging), and only **about 7% (~50) are websites**. Most of the web items are portfolio and studio sites (p074, p113, p114, p279, p280, p495–p506, p519–p528).
- **Presentation is a large confound.** Roughly 150–200 images (around 25%) show a document photographed as an object: a small spread or card floating on a **black mat** (very common: p035, p091, p209, p246, p295–p301, p352, p488, p510, p519–p531, p563, p586–p591, p612–p615, p663–p669, p688–p693), or a poster pasted in the street or subway (p072, p082, p087, p093, p128, p174, p363, p373, p443, p465, p653, p696). These are documentation habits, not compositions. In a still image they read as "a small rectangle in a black void", which can pollute similarity searches. See the "Black Mat Presentation" note at the end.
- **Taste profile.** Restrained, contemporary independent-studio work (Swiss, Dutch, Belgian, Korean and LA indies, 2016–2024) on Tumblr, Savee and It's Nice That. Grotesk sans with a few didone and serif accents. Lots of white or neutral ground. Black, fluorescent yellow, green and blue are the few loud colours. Almost no illustration-led or ornamental work. The strong moves are **scale contrast** (huge vs tiny), **emptiness**, **rotation**, and **typographic structure standing in for imagery**.

## The styles (20)

### 1. Monumental Type Fill
- **Test:** One word or phrase is set so large that it touches or crops at two or more edges of the format. The type *is* the image, and anything else is small and subordinate.
- **Not:** Bookend Type (#2), where giant type sits only at the top and bottom with a middle zone. Also not Overprint Type Stack (#20), where several layers of type collide.
- **Count:** ~40
- **Strongest:** p017, p065, p144, p205, p226, p403, p442, p514, p585, p680
- **Web first screen:** A single headline at viewport width, cropped by the screen edges, with nav and meta in tiny type tucked into the leftover corners.
- **Search:** "oversized typography poster", "type as image full bleed", "giant headline cropped edges", "billboard typography layout", "edge to edge display type", "monumental grotesk poster"

### 2. Bookend Type
- **Test:** Two bands of giant type pin the top and bottom edges, and a smaller image or info block sits in the gap between them.
- **Not:** Monumental Type Fill (#1), where one mass fills the format. Also not Centered Classical Axis (#12), which is small type and symmetric.
- **Count:** ~12
- **Strongest:** p052, p062, p069, p119, p161, p588, p043, p303, p632
- **Web first screen:** Brand name at full width across the top of the viewport and a date or second word at full width across the bottom, with a photo or video floating between them.
- **Search:** "type top and bottom poster", "sandwich layout typography", "split headline top bottom", "header footer giant type", "bookended poster design"

### 3. Vertical Spine Type
- **Test:** A line of type is rotated 90° and runs the full height of the format as a structural column or edge, with other content hung against it.
- **Not:** Type on a Path (#19), which is diagonal or curved and expressive rather than structural. When the type runs around all four edges, see "Type Perimeter Frame" under smaller clusters.
- **Count:** ~40
- **Strongest:** p034, p041, p164, p312, p408, p442, p520, p551, p557, p666
- **Web first screen:** A rotated wordmark or section title fixed down the left or right edge of the viewport, acting like a spine, with content scrolling beside it.
- **Search:** "vertical typography poster", "rotated type layout", "sideways headline", "type running up the edge", "vertical text spine editorial", "90 degree typography"

### 4. Lone Object in Void
- **Test:** One photographed or cut-out object sits roughly centred in a large, flat, empty field (white, colour or black) and takes up less than about 30% of the area. Any type is minimal.
- **Not:** Whisper Margins (#5), where the centre is empty and the type sits at the edges. Also not Floating Archipelago (#18), which has many small items.
- **Count:** ~35
- **Strongest:** p044, p125, p211, p233, p283, p401, p411, p598, p683, p698
- **Web first screen:** A single product or object image centred on a solid background, surrounded by large negative space, with a tiny caption below.
- **Search:** "single object negative space poster", "isolated product on plain background layout", "object centered empty field", "minimal still life poster", "cutout object vast whitespace"

### 5. Whisper Margins
- **Test:** The centre of the format is empty or nearly empty, and all information lives in tiny type pushed into corners or along the edges, so the composition is the frame.
- **Not:** Lone Object in Void (#4), which has a centred focal object. Also not Ledger Composition (#6), which is dense text.
- **Count:** ~30
- **Strongest:** p048, p117, p124, p214, p324, p374, p439, p504, p572, p665
- **Web first screen:** An almost blank viewport with four tiny text fragments at the corners (name, nav, year, contact) and maybe one small dot or image.
- **Search:** "tiny type corners empty poster", "minimal poster small text edges", "negative space typography layout", "whitespace frame layout", "corner text minimal website"

### 6. Ledger Composition
- **Test:** The whole composition is a dense list, table or index (tracklists, schedules, credits, contents), set in columns and rows. Its rhythm stands in for an image.
- **Not:** Exposed Ruled Grid (#8), where visible boxes or rules carry mixed content. Also not Text Wall (#17), which is running prose set large.
- **Count:** ~40
- **Strongest:** p027, p148, p218, p255, p285, p346, p400, p420, p574, p654
- **Web first screen:** A full-screen index: rows of project, year and client in monospace or grotesk, rule lines between them, with no hero image.
- **Search:** "list typography poster", "tabular layout design", "index poster schedule typography", "tracklist poster design", "timetable graphic design", "data sheet typography"

### 7. Bleed Photo Overprint
- **Test:** A photograph fills the whole format edge to edge, and type is set directly on top of it (white or coloured), with no panel or box behind the type.
- **Not:** Split Field (#15), where photo and type get separate zones. Also not Layered Collage (#10), which uses multiple images.
- **Count:** ~45
- **Strongest:** p011, p130, p150, p203, p225, p273, p336, p423, p593, p651
- **Web first screen:** A full-bleed photo or video hero with the headline and nav overprinted in one colour straight onto the image.
- **Search:** "full bleed photo poster type overlay", "text over photograph poster", "photographic poster white type", "magazine cover overprint", "full screen image hero typography"

### 8. Exposed Ruled Grid
- **Test:** The underlying grid is drawn in: hairline rules, cells, boxes or crosshairs are visible, and type and images snap into the cells.
- **Not:** Colour Block Mosaic (#9), where the cells are filled with flat colour. Also not Specimen Matrix (#13), which is a uniform grid of many photos with no rules.
- **Count:** ~40
- **Strongest:** p139, p221, p274, p289, p368, p398, p617, p631, p654, p680
- **Web first screen:** Hairline-bordered cells across the viewport, like a spreadsheet or calendar, each holding a label, number or thumbnail.
- **Search:** "visible grid poster", "ruled lines layout design", "modular grid boxes typography", "swiss grid hairline rules", "calendar grid poster", "table structure graphic design"

### 9. Colour Block Mosaic
- **Test:** The format is divided into a few large rectangles of flat, saturated colour (Mondrian-like), with photos and type placed inside or against the blocks.
- **Not:** Exposed Ruled Grid (#8), which is line-based and neutral. Also not Split Field (#15), which has only two halves.
- **Count:** ~10
- **Strongest:** p190, p197, p198, p327, p364, p365, p388, p497, p622
- **Web first screen:** The viewport is tiled into three to five flat colour panels of unequal size, one holding a photo and the others holding a headline and links.
- **Search:** "colour block poster layout", "flat colour panels graphic design", "mondrian grid poster", "colour blocking editorial layout", "rectangular colour fields design"

### 10. Layered Collage
- **Test:** Several images, cut-outs and type fragments overlap each other at different scales and angles. There is no single grid, and depth comes from layering.
- **Not:** Floating Archipelago (#18), whose items don't overlap. Also not Specimen Matrix (#13), which is ordered.
- **Count:** ~35
- **Strongest:** p036, p088, p089, p098, p103, p372, p397, p416, p421, p625
- **Web first screen:** Overlapping image cards and stickers piled across the viewport, with type tucked between them and some elements rotated.
- **Search:** "layered collage poster", "overlapping images graphic design", "cut and paste layout", "mixed media collage editorial", "sticker collage design", "scrapbook poster layout"

### 11. Shape-Mask Window
- **Test:** A photo or colour field is cut into a non-rectangular shape (arch, oval, circle, blob or letterform) that sits as a cut-out window in a flat ground.
- **Not:** Lone Object in Void (#4), where the object's own silhouette makes the shape. Also not Colour Block Mosaic (#9), which uses rectangles.
- **Count:** ~20
- **Strongest:** p067, p073, p086, p158, p275, p302, p339, p356, p367, p508
- **Web first screen:** The hero image clipped into an arch, oval or blob on a solid background, with the headline set beside or across the shape.
- **Search:** "image in arch shape poster", "photo mask shape layout", "oval cutout graphic design", "blob shape poster", "clipped image shape editorial", "die cut window design"

### 12. Centered Classical Axis
- **Test:** All type and images are stacked on one central vertical axis, symmetric left to right, usually in modest-sized serif or spaced capitals with generous leading.
- **Not:** Bookend Type (#2), which uses giant type. Also not Whisper Margins (#5), which pushes type to the edges.
- **Count:** ~30
- **Strongest:** p105, p138, p175, p180, p216, p238, p331, p337, p353, p655
- **Web first screen:** A centred column with a title in serif or spaced caps, a small centred image and a centred date or link, all on a plain ground.
- **Search:** "centered symmetrical poster typography", "classical centered layout serif", "symmetric book cover design", "centered invitation typography", "axial layout design"

### 13. Specimen Matrix
- **Test:** Many small, equal-sized photos (10 or more) are repeated in a regular grid, like a contact sheet, catalogue or specimen tray, and the matrix *is* the composition.
- **Not:** Motif Field (#14), which repeats one graphic element. Also not Exposed Ruled Grid (#8), where the rules are visible and the content is mixed.
- **Count:** ~15
- **Strongest:** p097, p132, p177, p316, p358, p366, p406, p500, p507, p596
- **Web first screen:** An edge-to-edge grid of uniform thumbnails (products, faces, objects) with minimal labels, and nothing else above the fold.
- **Search:** "contact sheet layout", "grid of photos poster", "catalogue grid design", "typology photography grid", "specimen sheet layout", "uniform thumbnail grid"

### 14. Motif Field
- **Test:** One graphic element (a sticker, logo, blob, symbol or word) is repeated across the whole format as an all-over pattern or texture.
- **Not:** Specimen Matrix (#13), which varies the photos. Also not Monumental Type Fill (#1), which uses a single instance.
- **Count:** ~12
- **Strongest:** p129, p142, p200, p204, p230, p305, p334, p378, p474, p667
- **Web first screen:** A tiled or repeating logo or symbol pattern covering the background, with one message block over it.
- **Search:** "repeated logo pattern poster", "all over pattern graphic design", "repetition typography poster", "symbol pattern layout", "wallpaper logo identity"

### 15. Split Field
- **Test:** The format is cut into two clearly contrasting halves (photo vs flat colour, image vs text, or black vs white) along a straight vertical or horizontal line.
- **Not:** Colour Block Mosaic (#9), which uses more than two blocks. Also not Bleed Photo Overprint (#7), where one field carries both photo and type.
- **Count:** ~20
- **Strongest:** p008, p101, p202, p259, p387, p499, p553, p619, p633, p677
- **Web first screen:** A 50/50 viewport with a full-height photo on one side and a flat colour field holding the headline on the other.
- **Search:** "split screen poster design", "half image half colour layout", "diptych editorial layout", "two panel poster", "split composition graphic design"

### 16. Type-Image Rebus
- **Test:** Small images sit *inline* within lines of large type, replacing or punctuating words, so text and image read as one sentence.
- **Not:** Layered Collage (#10), which overlaps without a reading line. Also not Bleed Photo Overprint (#7).
- **Count:** ~13
- **Strongest:** p054, p059, p215, p223, p294, p432, p463, p545, p587, p635
- **Web first screen:** One large sentence headline with small inline thumbnails embedded between the words.
- **Search:** "images inline with text typography", "picture in headline layout", "rebus typography poster", "inline image headline website", "text and image interwoven"

### 17. Text Wall
- **Test:** A block of running prose (not a list) is set large and justified or ragged, and fills most of the format like a texture. Any image is small or sits under the text.
- **Not:** Ledger Composition (#6), which is tabular. Also not Monumental Type Fill (#1), which is a few words.
- **Count:** ~12
- **Strongest:** p010, p111, p267, p340, p342, p396, p492, p662, p675
- **Web first screen:** A manifesto paragraph at about 4–6vw filling the viewport, cropping off the bottom edge, with no image.
- **Search:** "big paragraph poster", "text block as image", "manifesto typography layout", "large body text design", "justified text wall poster"

### 18. Floating Archipelago
- **Test:** Many small images and text fragments are scattered across a mostly empty field at irregular positions and sizes. They don't overlap and there is no visible grid.
- **Not:** Layered Collage (#10), whose items overlap. Also not Lone Object in Void (#4), which has one item, and Specimen Matrix (#13), which is regular.
- **Count:** ~18
- **Strongest:** p015, p121, p179, p201, p217, p290, p511, p513, p518, p671
- **Web first screen:** Small images and captions drift at scattered coordinates across a white canvas, like a moodboard, with lots of air between them.
- **Search:** "scattered images layout", "floating images whitespace website", "asymmetric scattered editorial", "moodboard layout design", "free placement images portfolio"

### 19. Type on a Path
- **Test:** Display type follows a diagonal, curve or circle (around an object, along an arc, or tilted across the frame), giving the composition visible motion.
- **Not:** Vertical Spine Type (#3), which is orthogonal and structural. Also not Overprint Type Stack (#20).
- **Count:** ~13
- **Strongest:** p100, p219, p252, p296, p404, p471, p490, p580, p333, p240
- **Web first screen:** A headline set on an arc or steep diagonal across the viewport, wrapping around a central image.
- **Search:** "type on a curve poster", "diagonal typography layout", "circular text poster", "type following path design", "tilted headline poster"

### 20. Overprint Type Stack
- **Test:** Several layers of type are printed over each other or ghosted behind each other (misregistered, overlapping or tinted), so legibility is partly sacrificed for density.
- **Not:** Monumental Type Fill (#1), which is a single clean layer. Also not Layered Collage (#10), which is image-led.
- **Count:** ~12
- **Strongest:** p104, p115, p213, p270, p271, p390, p409, p429, p608
- **Web first screen:** Overlapping headline layers in two tints, one ghosted at large scale behind a crisp smaller layer.
- **Search:** "overlapping typography poster", "overprinted type layers", "ghosted type background", "layered letters design", "misregistered type poster"

## Smaller but striking clusters (<10, worth tracking)

- **Type Perimeter Frame** (~8): type runs around all four edges like a border, and the image sits in the middle. p141, p168, p185, p248, p457, p548, p649, p338. Web: a running marquee or edge-text frame around the viewport.
- **Letter Scatter** (~8): the letters of one word are spread out individually across the field or a grid. p584, p627, p462, p659, p483, p389, p221, p407. Web: a wordmark exploded across the viewport.
- **Script Ribbon** (~7): one oversized calligraphic or script line sweeps across a photo or field. p081, p153, p154, p349, p350, p351, p297.
- **Typographic Environment** (~3): type applied to architecture or rooms. p678, p162, p559.
- **Black Mat Presentation** (~150+, documentation): a spread or card photographed small on black. It is *visually* a strong move (a small bright rectangle in a black void) and is effectively Black-Page Drama. However, it is a presentation habit, so it should be excluded or down-weighted rather than treated as a style.

## Discarded or merged from the suggested list

- "Tiny type in corners framing an empty centre" was kept as **Whisper Margins**.
- "Single object isolated in a vast empty field" was kept as **Lone Object in Void**.
- "Repetition/pattern of one element" was split into **Motif Field** (graphic element) and **Specimen Matrix** (many photos), because they look different at thumbnail size.
- "Visible grid / modular rules" was split into **Exposed Ruled Grid** and **Colour Block Mosaic**.
- "Type at architectural scale" was split into **Monumental Type Fill** and **Bookend Type**.
- New styles not on the suggested list: **Type-Image Rebus**, **Text Wall**, **Floating Archipelago**, **Type on a Path**, **Overprint Type Stack**.

|---|---|---|
| Monumental Type Fill | **Monumental Type**, Expressive Typographic | **Duplicate of Monumental Type.** Keep one. |
| Bookend Type | Monumental Type, Strict Modular Swiss | Sub-variant. Distinct enough to keep. |
| Vertical Spine Type | Late Swiss New Wave, Neo-Swiss Archive | Cuts across both. Keep. |
